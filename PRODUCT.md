# Product

## Register

product

## Users

THIÊN LỘC GROUP serves doctors, hospitals, clinics, laboratories, medical centers, procurement teams, technicians, equipment dealers, veterinary clinics, veterinary hospitals, sales staff and administrators. On the public catalog, users need to identify suitable equipment, compare decision-critical information and contact the company without navigating marketing noise.

## Product Purpose

The website helps professional buyers and clinical users discover medical and veterinary equipment by field, category, specialty, brand and application. Success means users can understand where they are, narrow a large catalog efficiently, scan key product information and preserve their search state while moving toward consultation or quotation.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, professional and trustworthy. The tone is modern, technical and calm, with enough polish to signal quality without reducing readability or task speed.

## Anti-references

- Marketplace interfaces that resemble Shopee or use price-led promotional pressure.
- Generic SaaS landing pages, startup templates, crypto aesthetics and AI-generated card grids.
- Decorative glass, glow, gradients, oversized headings, excessive badges and repeated centered layouts.
- Veterinary interfaces that resemble a pet shop or rely on cute animal imagery.
- Claims, customer names, certifications, distribution relationships or precise business figures without verified data.

## Design Principles

1. Clarity before decoration.
2. Readability before effects.
3. Information before marketing noise.
4. Evidence before claims.
5. Familiar discovery patterns before novel controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Important content uses at least 14px text, with 16px as the default reading size. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, high-contrast controls, reduced motion, clear Vietnamese labels and comfortable touch targets are required. Forms must not use placeholders as labels. The interface must remain usable for older users and for users scanning dense technical information.
